# Explore: Trip Header Redesign

## What was explored
The trip detail page header (visible in Round 8 screenshots). Currently: a tall purple/gradient hero box with a floating flag emoji centred in it, a back button top-left, and a status badge bottom-left. Country name, dates, and trip meta sit below the purple zone in the white content area.

## The problem
- Flag floating centred in a blank purple box looks odd and wastes vertical space
- The hero zone carries no useful information — it's decoration with a navigation element
- Country name is separated from its visual identity (the flag)
- Status badge placement at the bottom of the hero is easy to miss

## Direction chosen
Three parallel design pitches were requested. Ben to choose one. Directions explored:
1. Compact functional bar — collapse the hero, move everything into a single tight header
2. Information-rich hero — keep the zone, make it earn its space by containing the country name + key trip info
3. Visual destination card — use the hero as a travel-app-style destination canvas with flag integrated small

## Underlying principles agreed on
- The header must communicate: where (destination), when (dates), and status — without requiring the user to scroll
- The flag should be an identifier, not a centrepiece
- Every element needs a functional reason to exist at the top of the page

## Open questions
- Whether the purple gradient stays as brand colour or gets replaced with something contextual (e.g. destination image, status-driven colour)
- Whether the country name belongs in the hero zone or just below it
